# Code of conduct

**English** · [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Dev Team Office to be a good experience for everyone: in its issues, its pull requests, its discussions and anywhere else the project is represented. Whatever your age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, religion or sexual identity and orientation, you are welcome here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to programming.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before you conclude.
- Give and receive feedback about the work, never about the person.
- When you make a mistake, own it and fix it.
- Think about what is best for the people who use the office, not only about being right.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or demeaning comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information (an address, an email, a name they did not share) without their permission.
- Trolling, and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, tell the maintainer privately, through the contact on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed quickly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected. Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: they go through the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## What happens next

The maintainer decides what to do about behavior that goes against this code, from a private word to a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces, and may remove comments, commits, code or issues that break it. This applies to everyone, maintainers included.
