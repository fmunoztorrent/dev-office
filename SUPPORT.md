# Getting help

**English** · [Español](SUPPORT.es.md)

## First, the docs

- **It does not install, or an agent does not show up:** `dev-team-office doctor` says what is missing and how to fix it. Then the [troubleshooting table](docs/installation.md#troubleshooting).
- **How do I…?** The [usage guide](docs/usage.md) covers the day to day, and the [index of the docs](docs/README.md) everything else.
- **What changed in a version:** the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md).

## Then, an issue

[Open an issue](https://github.com/fmunoztorrent/dev-office/issues/new/choose): a bug, an idea, or a question the docs do not answer (a question that comes up is a gap in the docs). For a bug, include:

- what you did, what you expected and what happened;
- the output of `dev-team-office doctor`;
- your Node version (`node -v`) and your agent's (Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Pi…);
- for something in the game, a screenshot and which view you were in (front or top-down).

## Never in public

A security vulnerability goes through the [security policy](SECURITY.md), privately.
